# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in SillCast a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize, and repair harm when mistakes happen.
- Keep discussions focused on the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, sexualized language, discriminatory comments, doxxing, deliberate
disruption, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the security contact
route in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments,
commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or
permanently ban contributors.

This compact policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
